# 🌆 What is UrbanSync?

**UrbanSync** is a centralized **Urban Traffic & Transport Operations Management Platform built on ServiceNow**.

Modern cities generate traffic-related incidents continuously — accidents, road blockages, congestion, vehicle breakdowns, road maintenance, diversions and other transportation disruptions. Managing these situations often involves multiple agencies, traffic control teams, field personnel, routes and resources.

UrbanSync brings these operational activities into a **single connected ServiceNow platform**.

Instead of handling traffic incidents through disconnected communication channels and manually maintained records, UrbanSync provides a structured workflow where an incident can be:

> **Reported → Recorded → Classified → Assigned → Responded → Monitored → Resolved → Analyzed**

The platform is designed around the concept of a **Traffic Management Center (TMC)**, providing operators with a centralized view of incidents, agencies, routes, resources and ongoing traffic operations.

# 🗄️ Data Model & Custom Tables

UrbanSync uses **ServiceNow Custom Tables** to organize operational information required for traffic incident management.

The major data domains defined for the application include:

| # | Data Entity | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Traffic Incidents** | Stores incident information such as type, location, severity, priority, status, and related details |
| 2 | **Agencies** | Maintains information about Traffic Police, Ambulance, Fire, Road Maintenance, and Public Transport authorities |
| 3 | **Field Teams** | Maintains field-team information, availability, and assignments |
| 4 | **Traffic Assets** | Maintains traffic signals and other infrastructure information |
| 5 | **Transport Disruptions** | Maintains information about affected transport services, disruptions, and diversions |

Traffic infrastructure information can also be associated with **ServiceNow CMDB** for centralized asset monitoring and maintenance.

> **Note:** The exact custom-table count and ServiceNow table names will be updated here as implementation progresses.

# 🔄 System Workflow

UrbanSync follows an end-to-end incident management workflow.

```text
Citizen / Field Officer / CCTV / Traffic Sensor
                     │
                     ▼
              Incident Report
                     │
                     ▼
           Incident Registration
                     │
                     ▼
              AI Assessment
                     │
                     ▼
        Incident Prioritization
                     │
                     ▼
           Resource Assignment
                     │
                     ▼
        Multi-Agency Coordination
                     │
                     ▼
             Field Response
                     │
                     ▼
        Real-Time Status Updates
                     │
                     ▼
          Incident Resolution
                     │
                     ▼
          Reports & Analytics
```

### Workflow Steps

**1. Report Incident**  
A citizen, commuter, or field officer reports a traffic incident.

**2. Incident Registration**  
The incident information is received, verified, and recorded.

**3. AI Assessment**  
AI-assisted capabilities analyze the incident and support severity assessment, impact analysis, and response recommendations.

**4. Incident Prioritization**  
The incident is classified and prioritized based on available information and operational policies.

**5. Resource Assignment**  
Relevant agencies and response teams are assigned.

**6. Response & Coordination**  
Assigned agencies acknowledge their tasks, perform response activities, and update incident progress.

**7. Progress Monitoring**  
The Traffic Management Centre monitors the incident lifecycle and field updates.

**8. Incident Resolution**  
Once the situation is resolved, the incident is closed and relevant stakeholders are notified.

**9. Reporting & Analytics**  
Operational data contributes to dashboards, KPIs, reports, and future analysis.

# 🚨 Example Scenario

Consider a major road accident occurring during peak morning traffic.

### 1️⃣ Incident Reporting
A citizen or field officer reports the accident through the Service Portal or mobile interface.

### 2️⃣ Incident Assessment
The incident reaches the Traffic Management Centre. The system assists in analyzing its severity and possible impact.

### 3️⃣ Multi-Agency Response
Relevant agencies such as Traffic Police, Ambulance Services, Road Maintenance, and Public Transport authorities are notified.

### 4️⃣ Traffic Coordination
Authorities coordinate traffic diversions and transport-route changes when necessary.

### 5️⃣ Field Response
Field teams respond to the incident and update progress.

### 6️⃣ Monitoring
TMC operators monitor the incident and agency activities through the centralized platform.

### 7️⃣ Resolution
Once the incident is resolved and the road is cleared, the incident can be closed and relevant stakeholders notified.

### 8️⃣ Analytics
Incident information contributes to dashboards, reports, and operational analysis.
