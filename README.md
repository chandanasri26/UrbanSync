# 🌆 What is UrbanSync?

**UrbanSync** is a centralized **Urban Traffic & Transport Operations Management Platform built on ServiceNow**.

Modern cities experience traffic-related incidents such as accidents, vehicle breakdowns, traffic signal failures, road and infrastructure damage, public events, adverse weather conditions, congestion, and public transport disruptions.

Managing these incidents often requires coordination between multiple organizations, including:

- Traffic Management Centre (TMC)
- Traffic Police
- Ambulance & Emergency Services
- Fire Department
- Road Maintenance Teams
- Traffic Signal Maintenance Teams
- Public Transport Authorities
- Field Officers
- Citizens & Commuters

UrbanSync brings these stakeholders and operational activities into a **single connected ServiceNow platform**.

Instead of managing incidents through disconnected communication channels, phone calls, emails, and isolated systems, UrbanSync provides a structured incident lifecycle:

> **Report → Register → Assess → Prioritize → Assign → Respond → Monitor → Resolve → Analyze**

The platform is designed around a centralized **Traffic Management Centre (TMC)** where operators can monitor incidents, agency responses, field activities, traffic infrastructure, and transport disruptions.

# 🎯 The Problem UrbanSync Solves

Urban traffic management involves several interconnected components:

- 🚨 Traffic incidents
- 🏢 Government and emergency agencies
- 🚓 Emergency and field resources
- 🛣️ Traffic routes
- 🔀 Route diversions
- 👷 Field personnel
- 🚦 Traffic infrastructure
- 📱 Citizen reports
- ⚙️ Operational workflows
- 📊 Traffic and incident data

When these components operate through disconnected systems, incident management can become slow and difficult to coordinate.

Typical challenges include:

- Fragmented communication between agencies
- Manual incident assessment and dispatch
- Delayed emergency response
- Lack of unified operational visibility
- Manual public transport diversion management
- Limited citizen communication
- Reactive infrastructure maintenance
- Limited traffic and operational analytics

A traditional process may look like:

```text
Incident Report
      │
      ▼
Manual Verification
      │
      ▼
Contact Different Agencies
      │
      ▼
Find Available Resources
      │
      ▼
Coordinate Response
      │
      ▼
Manage Traffic / Diversions
      │
      ▼
Update Multiple Systems
      │
      ▼
Resolve Incident
```

UrbanSync replaces this fragmented approach with a centralized and automated ServiceNow workflow.

# ✨ Key Features

UrbanSync combines incident management, workflow automation, multi-agency coordination, citizen services, infrastructure management, and analytics into a unified traffic operations platform.

### 🚨 Centralized Incident Management

- Report and manage traffic incidents from a single platform
- Maintain incident information such as type, location, severity, priority, and status
- Track incidents throughout their lifecycle
- Provide centralized visibility to Traffic Management Centre operators

### 🤝 Multi-Agency Coordination

- Coordinate Traffic Police, Ambulance Services, Fire Department, Road Maintenance, and Public Transport authorities
- Route incident-related activities to appropriate agencies
- Reduce dependency on manual communication
- Maintain coordinated status updates throughout the incident lifecycle

### 🤖 AI-Assisted Incident Assessment

- Assist with incident analysis
- Support severity and impact assessment
- Help prioritize incidents
- Recommend possible response actions
- Keep operators involved in validating important AI recommendations

### 🚑 Smart Resource Allocation

- Maintain information about field teams and available resources
- Support assignment of appropriate emergency and maintenance teams
- Consider incident priority and location during resource assignment
- Track assigned teams throughout the response process

### 🛣️ Traffic & Diversion Management

- Identify traffic disruptions caused by incidents
- Support alternate-route recommendations
- Coordinate traffic diversions
- Assist authorities during major road disruptions

### 🚌 Public Transport Coordination

- Track public transport disruptions
- Coordinate with transport authorities
- Support route diversions during accidents or road closures
- Provide operational updates during disruptions

### 📱 Citizen Service Portal

- Allow citizens to report traffic incidents
- Provide a structured incident-reporting interface
- Allow citizens to track incident status
- Provide relevant incident and traffic updates

### 👷 Mobile Field Operations

- Support field officers through Now Mobile
- Allow field teams to update incident progress
- Provide operational information to personnel working at incident locations
- Improve coordination between field teams and the Traffic Management Centre

### 🔧 Traffic Infrastructure Management

- Maintain traffic signal and infrastructure information
- Use CMDB for infrastructure asset information
- Track maintenance history
- Support preventive maintenance activities

### 🔔 Automated Notifications & Escalations

- Send incident-related notifications to stakeholders
- Generate alerts when important status changes occur
- Support task escalation through automated workflows
- Keep relevant agencies informed during incident handling

### 📊 Dashboards & Performance Analytics

- Monitor active and resolved incidents
- Track response and resolution information
- Analyze incident trends
- Monitor operational KPIs
- Provide dashboards and reports for city authorities
