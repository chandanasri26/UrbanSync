# 🌆 What is UrbanSync?

**UrbanSync** is a centralized **Urban Traffic & Transport Operations Management Platform built on ServiceNow**.

Modern cities generate traffic-related incidents continuously — accidents, road blockages, congestion, vehicle breakdowns, road maintenance, diversions and other transportation disruptions. Managing these situations often involves multiple agencies, traffic control teams, field personnel, routes and resources.

UrbanSync brings these operational activities into a **single connected ServiceNow platform**.

Instead of handling traffic incidents through disconnected communication channels and manually maintained records, UrbanSync provides a structured workflow where an incident can be:

> **Reported → Recorded → Classified → Assigned → Responded → Monitored → Resolved → Analyzed**

The platform is designed around the concept of a **Traffic Management Center (TMC)**, providing operators with a centralized view of incidents, agencies, routes, resources and ongoing traffic operations.

# ✨ Key Features

UrbanSync combines incident management, workflow automation, multi-agency coordination, citizen services and analytics into a unified traffic operations platform.

### 🚨 Centralized Incident Management
- Report and manage traffic incidents from a single platform
- Maintain incident type, location, severity, priority and status
- Track an incident throughout its complete lifecycle
- Provide centralized visibility to Traffic Management Centre operators

### 🤝 Multi-Agency Coordination
- Coordinate Traffic Police, Ambulance Services, Fire Department, Road Maintenance and Public Transport authorities
- Automatically route incident-related tasks to appropriate teams
- Reduce dependency on manual communication between departments
- Maintain status updates across participating agencies

### 🤖 AI-Assisted Incident Assessment
- Assist in analyzing reported traffic incidents
- Support incident severity and impact assessment
- Help prioritize incidents based on operational information
- Recommend possible response actions
- Keep operators involved in validating important decisions

### 🚑 Smart Resource Allocation
- Maintain information about available field resources
- Assign appropriate emergency and maintenance teams
- Support resource allocation based on incident priority and location
- Track assigned teams during incident response

### 🛣️ Traffic & Diversion Management
- Maintain traffic route information
- Identify routes affected by incidents
- Support alternate-route and diversion management
- Coordinate diversions during major traffic disruptions

### 🚌 Public Transport Coordination
- Track transport disruptions caused by traffic incidents
- Coordinate with public transport authorities
- Support bus route diversion during road closures or accidents
- Provide operational updates during disruptions

### 📱 Citizen Service Portal
- Allow citizens to report traffic incidents
- Provide a structured incident-reporting interface
- Allow users to track incident progress
- Provide relevant incident and traffic updates

### 👷 Mobile Field Operations
- Support field officers through Now Mobile
- Allow field teams to update incident progress
- Provide operational information to teams working on-site
- Improve communication between field personnel and the Traffic Management Centre

### 🔧 Traffic Infrastructure Management
- Maintain traffic signal and infrastructure information
- Use CMDB to manage traffic-related assets
- Track maintenance information
- Support preventive infrastructure maintenance

### 🔔 Automated Notifications & Escalations
- Send incident-related notifications to stakeholders
- Generate alerts when important status changes occur
- Support task escalation through automated workflows
- Keep relevant agencies informed throughout the incident lifecycle

### 📊 Dashboards & Performance Analytics
- Monitor active and resolved incidents
- Track response and resolution information
- Analyze recurring incident patterns
- Monitor operational KPIs
- Provide centralized dashboards for city authorities

# 🛠️ Technologies Used

UrbanSync is primarily developed using the **ServiceNow platform**, supported by web technologies, integrations and design tools.

| Category | Technology | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| ☁️ **Platform** | **ServiceNow** | Core platform for developing and managing UrbanSync |
| 🏗️ **Application Development** | **App Engine Studio** | Build the UrbanSync custom application |
| 🗄️ **Data Management** | **Custom Tables** | Store incidents, agencies, field teams, traffic assets and transport disruptions |
| ⚙️ **Workflow Automation** | **Flow Designer** | Automate incident response and multi-agency workflows |
| 🧠 **Business Logic** | **Business Rules** | Execute validations and backend automation |
| 🔔 **Notifications** | **ServiceNow Notifications** | Send alerts and incident status updates |
| 📱 **Citizen Interface** | **Service Portal** | Citizen-facing incident reporting and tracking portal |
| 📲 **Mobile Operations** | **Now Mobile** | Support field officers and mobile incident updates |
| 🚨 **Incident Operations** | **Major Incident Management** | Coordinate critical traffic incidents |
| 🔧 **Infrastructure Management** | **CMDB** | Maintain traffic signals and infrastructure asset information |
| 🤖 **AI Assistance** | **Virtual Agent / Now Assist** | Assist citizens and operators with incident-related interactions |
| 📊 **Analytics** | **Performance Analytics** | Dashboards, KPIs, incident trends and operational performance |
